1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Larry The Barber — Grooming Co. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, contact us, purchase products, or book an appointment. By using our site or services, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically:

  • Contact & booking details — name, phone number, email, and appointment preferences you submit when booking or contacting us.
  • Transaction details — records of services and products purchased (payment card data is handled by our payment and booking providers, not stored by us).
  • Usage data — pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, remind you of, and manage your appointments.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer service.
  • To process product purchases and fulfill orders.
  • To improve our website, services, and offerings.
  • To send service updates and, where permitted, promotional messages (you can opt out anytime).
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud.

4. Online booking & third parties

Online appointments are powered by Booksy, a third-party scheduling platform. When you book through our site you are directed to Booksy, and the information you provide there is handled under Booksy's privacy policy. We may also use payment processors and analytics providers. These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (booking, payments, hosting, analytics).
  • Professional advisors and authorities where required by law.
  • A successor entity in the event of a business transfer.

6. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand usage. For details and your choices, see our Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing or marketing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

8. Data retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Appointments for minors are arranged by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
