Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Our commitment

Larry The Barber is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every visitor to be able to learn about our services, view our products, and book an appointment with ease.

Standards we aim for

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological needs.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML and descriptive headings for clear structure.
  • Alt text on meaningful images.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, links, and buttons.
  • Responsive layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops.
  • Clear, descriptive link and button labels.

Booking accessibility

Our online booking is provided through Booksy. If you experience any difficulty booking online, you are always welcome to call or text us at (404) 490-3052 and we will gladly schedule your appointment for you.

Known limitations

Some third-party content (such as embedded booking tools or maps) may not be fully under our control. We work to choose providers that prioritize accessibility and continually review our own content.

Feedback & assistance

We welcome your feedback. If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, or need information in a different format, please contact us:

  • Phone / Text: (404) 490-3052
  • Business: Larry The Barber, Atlanta, GA

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within a reasonable timeframe.
