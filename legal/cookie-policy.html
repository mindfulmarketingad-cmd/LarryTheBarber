1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage, which we refer to collectively as "cookies" here.

2. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to work — for example, page navigation and security. These cannot be switched off in our systems.

Preference

Remember choices you make (such as your region) to give you a more personalized experience.

Analytics / performance

Help us understand which pages are visited and how the site performs so we can improve it. These collect aggregated, mostly anonymous information.

Marketing

May be set if we run promotions or advertising, to measure effectiveness. We only use these where permitted and, where required, with your consent.

3. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties whose services we use, including:

  • Booksy — our online booking platform, when you start or complete a booking.
  • Google Fonts — used to load the site's typefaces.
  • Analytics providers — if enabled, to measure site usage.

These third parties process data under their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing your preferences

Most browsers let you view, manage, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works. Helpful links:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our practices or the technologies we use change. The "Last updated" date shows the latest revision.
